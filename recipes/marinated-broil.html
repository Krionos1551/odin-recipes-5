Pacific Style Marinated London Broil

Description

This Recipe is origonally for Korean Style Short Ribs but has been stolen for London Broil, as the broil is about 5X cheaper than shortribs

London Broil is usually a tough cut of meat, but by cutting it into strips agains the grain, this becomes a nonissue

Ingrediants

  • London Broil
  • 1/4 cup Sugar
  • 1 Korean Pear
  • 1 Honeycrisp Apple
  • 1 Small Yellow Onion
  • 1/2 - 3/4 Cup Pinapple Juice
  • 1/2 Cup Soy Sauce
  • 4 Tbsp Mirin
  • 2 Tbsp Sesame Oil
  • 8 Cloves of Garlic
  • 1 Tbsp Fresh Ginger
  • 2 Tbsp Brown Sugar
  • 2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 Stalks Green Onion

Procedure

  1. Slice London Broil against the grain of the meat, the thinner the strips the better
  2. Fill a large bowl with water and sugar, add sliced meat to the bowl and let sit for 15-20 minutes.
  3. Rough Cut all the remaining large Ingrediants, (Apple, Pear, Yellow Onion)
  4. Add all remaining Ingrediants (EXCEPT the Green Onion) to a blender and blend until smooth
  5. Once Throughly blended, strain through a siev to remove all pulp
  6. Remove Broil from water and discard the water.
  7. Add Broil to a gallon zip-loc bag and add in strained liquid
  8. Let sit for 3 days, picking up and agitating the bag once a day
  9. Cook on a grill or frying pan till desired doneness
