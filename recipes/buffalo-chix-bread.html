Buffalo Chicken Pull Apart Bread

Description

This Recipe is delisious and can easily be adjusted to any spicy level for anyone.

While it is a little involved the payoff is definetly worth the wait. The basic method is that you will be alternating placeing Chicken and Pilliburry dough

This Recipe will take aproximatly an hour to prepare and another 30-45 minutes to bake fully.

Ingridients

  • 2 Cans Pillsburry Dough
  • 2lbs Shreaded Chicken
  • 1-1.5 cups cheese (cheddar is reccomended but you can use whatever)
  • Hot Sauce to taste
  • 1Tb Gran Garlic
  • 1Tb Salt
  • 1tsp Minced Chives

Procedure

  1. Preheat Oven to 350*F
  2. Mix Chicken, Cheese, Hot Sauce, Garlic and Salt together in a bowl.
  3. Remove Pillsburry dough from the tube and lay it flat
  4. Cut Dough in 2in^2 Squares
  5. Take a tablespoon of the Chicken mixture and place it on top of each dough square
  6. Place each square one after the other in a greesed bread pan
  7. Optional: Sprinkle extra cheese and a dash of Hot Sauce over top
  8. Put Bread pan in the oven and let bake for 45 minutes
  9. Check if it is done by poking a butter knife into the center of the loaf, if it comes out hot to the touch the Bread is done Baking
  10. Let Bread sit in the bread pan for 10 minutes before serving
  11. When Removed from the pan sprinkle chives on top then serve
