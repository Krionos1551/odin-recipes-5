Chicken Marsala

Description

This is a recipe that was taught to me by my mother and it was taught to her by her mother, so its been in the family for a while now

The most difficult part will be ensureing you make enough roux for all the chicken and bread

Ingridients

  • 2lbs Chicken Breast
  • .5 Cups Flour (or more as needed)
  • 2 Cups Marsala Wine
  • 2 Cups Chicken Stock
  • 1Tb Rosemary
  • .5tsp Salt
  • Pillsburry Crecent Rolls

Procedure

  1. Cut Chicken into 1 inch cubes
  2. Coat the bottom of a pan in vegtable oil and heat on a low High setting
  3. Put handfull of Chicken in a gallon zip-loc bag and add flower
  4. Shake Bag till the chicken is compleatly coated
  5. Cook Chicken in pan till cooked through and the breading is golden brown remove Chicken from pan
  6. Repeat Steps 3-5 untill all Chicken is cooked
  7. Once all Chicken is cooked, make sure the pan is hot and add marsala wine, let most of the wine boil off
  8. Around here is when you want to start baking the Crecent Rolls
  9. When most of the Wine is cooked off add an equal amount of chicken stock, the Rosemary and the Salt as well as any other seasoning. Let simmar on medium heat until half as thick as desired
  10. If Sauce does not thicken on its own, add a pinch of flour and wait 2 minutes reapeat till happy.
  11. Add chicken to the Sauce and let simmar until desired consistancy
